# Sign-in

::: info Community
The sign-in page is public.
:::

Enter username and password. After sign-in, the dashboard opens. English and فارسی can be selected on this page.

There are two roles:

- **Super Admin** — all Community areas, including Admins, Panels, Migration, and Settings
- **Reseller** — Dashboard, Clients, and their own traffic ledger. Global Settings, Panels, and Migration are hidden
