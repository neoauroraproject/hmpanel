# Backup Center

::: warning Premium
Enable the Backup Center module in Premium Settings. This page is for Super Admin.
:::

Backup Center is the scheduled edition. One-off snapshots remain under **Settings → Backup**.

On this page you can:

- See last-backup health, next run, and storage
- Schedule hourly, every six hours, every twelve hours, daily, every three days, or weekly
- Choose type: full, database only, configuration only, or a 3x-ui panel database
- Download, restore, or delete from history
- Review the Telegram delivery log
- Upload an archive, preview it, then confirm restore

Long-running jobs appear in Job Center inside Premium Settings.
