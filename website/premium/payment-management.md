# Payment Management

::: warning Premium
Enable the Payment Management module in Premium Settings.
:::

This is the central place for payment methods. Store and Admin Recharge use it to decide what the customer sees.

## Methods

Turn each method on or off. A disabled method is hidden from the customer. Some rows are coming soon and are only placeholders.

## Cards

Bank name, card number, holder, IBAN, and deposit instructions. These cards are shown wherever card-to-card is assigned.

## Assignments

For each area, choose which methods are allowed and which is the default:

- Store
- Add balance (Admin Recharge)
- Subscription renewal

If card-to-card is on, pick one card or all active cards.

## Transactions

Recorded payments: method, area, amount, status, and time.

## Settings

### Telegram Stars

Pay with Stars inside Telegram. Invoices use XTR. Delivery happens only after the panel confirms. Bind the store bot or the platform bot, and set Stars per USD and per Toman.

### Telegram Wallet (Wallet Pay)

The customer pays with TON, USDT, BTC, or NOT inside Telegram Wallet. A numeric Telegram user id is required (bot, Mini App, or linked portal). It is not shown on an anonymous web shop. Invoice currency is USD or EUR; Toman uses the conversion rate you set.
