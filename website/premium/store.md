# Store

::: warning Premium
Enable the Store module in Premium Settings. Branding should also be on for the storefront appearance.
:::

The Store administers orders, products, customers, and the public shop from one page. There are four main tabs.

## Overview

Sales summary and revenue charts. Recent orders are visible here.

## Commerce

- **Orders:** approve, reject, provision, cancel, or deliver manually
- **Products:** sellable plans with price and duration
- **Categories:** grouping on the storefront
- **Provisioning profiles:** which panel, traffic, and expiry a product creates — including 3x-ui, Eylan, and Pasarguard
- **Coupons:** limited to new purchases, renewals, or both
- **Wallet:** customer deposits, approve or reject
- **Add-ons:** IP limits and product extras for Eylan and Pasarguard

## Customers

The customer directory, attached services, and Telegram broadcast from the store bot.

## Settings

### Profile

Store name, storefront address, and the short text customers see.

### Store currency

Prices show in Toman or USD. The dashboard and catalog prefer this currency. If that price is empty, the other currency is shown.

### Subscription link type

After purchase, give the HMPanel link or the native 3x-ui link.

### Auto deliver

If on, after a receipt and the delay, the order is approved and the client is created automatically.

### Payment methods

Which ways the customer can pay. You can switch them here; full setup — cards, Telegram Stars, Wallet Pay, and which methods Store or Add Balance use — is in **Payment Management**.

### Card to card

The customer sees a bank card, pays, and uploads a receipt. You approve or reject the order.

### Customer wallet

The customer has a store balance. They can top it up and pay with it.

### Telegram bot

Buy and renew inside Telegram and the Mini App. Orders with a receipt are sent to the admin chat.

### Add-ons

Concurrent-user cap on a product (IP / HWID, according to 3x-ui, Pasarguard, or Eylan), plus Eylan / Pasarguard extras when Panel Plus is on.

## Customer side

Customers buy from the public shop, review orders and alerts in the portal, or follow the same path in the Telegram Mini App. Guest, portal, and Mini App checkout share one flow: category, product, name and add-ons, then payment.
